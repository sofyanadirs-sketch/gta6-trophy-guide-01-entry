    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official trophy descriptions alone: which story chapters and missions, which collectibles and counts, in what order. Stages overlap — start the Chapter 1 meditation in stage 3 while you play stage 2 — but the order below is the safe one.

      Missable trophies

      One trophy in the whole list can be missed. Everything else stays available after the credits, including the side-story finales.

      Save discipline

      • No mission replay, no New Game Plus. Once a story mission is done you cannot select it again, so a missed one-off stays missed for that playthrough.
      • Keep one manual save at the start of every chapter and one untouched save from just before the final mission. The game has 15 manual save slots plus one autosave.
      • Never use cheats on your trophy save. Cheats should switch off both trophies and saving.
      • The 100% checklist only appears after the story (Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion), but work you do earlier still counts toward it.

      Save rules: OutputLag — GTA 6 Trophy List. The only-missable flag: GTABase and PowerPyx via EGamers.
